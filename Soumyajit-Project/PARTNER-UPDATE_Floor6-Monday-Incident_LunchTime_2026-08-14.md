# FINBRIDGE PARTNERS: FLOOR 6 INCIDENT UPDATE
**Monday, 14 August 2026 – 12:30 PM**

---

## SUMMARY LINE FOR BUSY PARTNERS

This morning, Floor 6 experienced a login slowdown and one staff member reported an AI assistant may have shown confidential client information. Both issues are being treated seriously. The login problem is being resolved; the data access concern is under investigation by security specialists.

---

## A. WHAT HAPPENED

Two separate issues affected Floor 6 (Legal department, ~45 people) this morning:

**1. Login and Performance Problems**

Starting around 9:00 AM, many Floor 6 users found they could not log into their computers, or login took 5–10 minutes instead of the usual 30 seconds. We have identified the cause: a new document-management software was pushed to these machines on Friday afternoon, and that software is interfering with the normal login process.

**2. Data Access Concern**

One staff member was using an AI assistant (Microsoft's AI co-pilot) when she noticed it displayed confidential client information from a matter she believes she should not have access to. We are investigating whether this person did, in fact, have inappropriate access to confidential information. **This is not yet confirmed as a breach, and it is absolutely not being dismissed as a computer glitch.** It is being treated as a serious potential access-control problem.

---

## B. WHAT'S BEING DONE

**Login Issue – Already in Progress**

- We have confirmed the document-management software as the most likely cause.
- We have begun removing this software from Floor 6 machines.
- Machines receive this change at staggered times (not all at once), so login speed will improve gradually over the next 30–60 minutes as machines pick up the change.

**Data Access Concern – Under Investigation**

- This has been escalated immediately to FinBridge's security team and our data-protection officer.
- We are investigating whether the AI assistant should have been able to display that information, whether the staff member's account was misconfigured to have access it shouldn't have, and what happened and why.
- This is being handled carefully by the right specialists; we are not speculating.

---

## C. WHAT REMAINS UNCERTAIN & NEXT STEPS

- **Login recovery:** Depends on machines receiving the change. Most Floor 6 machines should be recovered **within 1 hour**; all should be recovered by early afternoon.
- **Data investigation:** Still in early stages. This will take longer than the login fix and requires careful review.
