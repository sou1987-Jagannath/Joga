## WHAT'S HAPPENING

If you're on Floor 6 and can't log in this morning, or logging in is taking 5–10 minutes instead of the usual 30 seconds, **we know about it.** A software update installed Friday afternoon is causing the problem.

---

## WHAT TO DO RIGHT NOW

### If You Haven't Logged In Yet
1. **Wait 15 minutes**, then try logging in again
2. Login should work normally this time (under 2 minutes)
3. If it still fails after 15 minutes, see "If It's Still Not Working" below

### If You're Already Logged In
1. **Keep working** — no need to do anything
2. Don't restart your computer
3. You're fine to stay logged in and continue with your day

---

## WHY THIS IS HAPPENING & WHAT WE'RE DOING

The software we installed Friday is causing your computer to hang when you log in. **We're removing that software right now** so it doesn't interfere with your login. Changes take about 15–30 minutes to reach your computer automatically. You don't need to restart or do anything — it happens in the background.

---

## IF IT'S STILL NOT WORKING

**After waiting 15–30 minutes, if login is still slow or failing:**

1. Try restarting your computer once
2. Wait 5 minutes
3. Try logging in again

**Still not fixed?** Contact IT Help Desk with these details:
- Your name and device name (usually on the sticker on your computer)
- The time you first saw the problem
- Whether you've restarted your computer

---

## WHEN TO EXPECT AN UPDATE

**We'll send a message by 10:00 AM** saying either "the problem is fixed" or "here's what we're doing next." 

Check your email or the IT intranet page for status updates.
