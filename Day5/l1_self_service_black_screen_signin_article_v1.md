# Black Screen After Sign-In: What To Do

Your files and data are safe.

Some users may see a black screen right after signing in. In most cases, it clears shortly after. If this happens, follow these steps.

## What you can do now
1. Wait for up to 60 seconds to see if the screen loads.
2. If it does not load, sign out.
3. Wait 30 seconds.
4. Sign in again.
5. If it still does not work, restart your computer once.
6. Sign in again after restart.

## If the issue continues
If you still see a black screen after trying the steps above, contact the Service Desk.

## What to include in your ticket
Please share these details so support can help you faster:
- Your full name and work login name
- Your office location or whether you are working from home
- The time the issue started (for example: 08:40 AM)
- What you see on screen (black screen, repeated sign-in screen, or you are suddenly signed out)
- Whether the problem happens every time or only sometimes
- What steps you already tried from this article
- Your computer name (if known)
- A screenshot or photo of the screen (if possible)

Your data is safe, and the team can restore access quickly once they have the details above.

Contact the Service Desk if the screen stays black after one sign-out/sign-in attempt and one restart.
