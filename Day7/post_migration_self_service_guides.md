# Post-Migration Self-Service Guides
**For FinBridge staff — plain language help for the most common issues after the Windows 11 move**

---

## 1. I can't log in — my account is locked

**Try this first:**
1. Wait 15 minutes and try logging in again without guessing your password. Repeated wrong attempts will keep the lock active.
2. If your organisation uses a self-service password reset page, visit it from your phone or another device and follow the steps to unlock your account.

**Still locked? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your full name and username
- The time the lockout happened
- Whether you got an error message (and what it said)
- How many times this has happened this week

## 2. My OneDrive files are missing

**Try this first:**
1. Click the OneDrive cloud icon in the bottom-right of your screen. If it shows a spinning circle or an error, your files are still syncing — give it 10 minutes and check again.
2. Search for the file by name using the search bar at the top of File Explorer. Files sometimes move to a slightly different folder after a migration.

**Still can't find your files? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and the file or folder name you're looking for
- When you last saw the file
- Whether the OneDrive icon shows an error symbol (red X or yellow warning)

📞 **Service Desk:** [insert number] | 📧 [insert email]

## 3. I can't open the S: shared drive

**Try this first:**
1. Open File Explorer, right-click on **This PC**, and select **Disconnect network drive** for S:, then restart your computer and try again.
2. If the S: drive is simply not listed, type `\\[server name]\finance` directly into the File Explorer address bar and press Enter.

> **Not sure of the server name?** Skip step 2 and go straight to the Service Desk.

**Still no access? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your name and job role
- The exact error message you see when you try to open S:
- Whether this is the first time you've tried since the migration

📞 **Service Desk:** [insert number] | 📧 [insert email]
